## In scope

- Letter + spelling picker, same roots as Chords. Scale picker instead of a free-typed name.
- Spelling on screen, with the key’s own accidentals.
- One relative line: major ↔ natural minor, and the pentatonic pair that shares those notes. Blues says it is the minor pentatonic plus the blue note.
- Two views: the whole neck (nut through the 12th fret, roots marked) and five CAGED boxes stepped with the same arrows, swipe, and arrow keys as Chords.
- Major uses the five fingered CAGED boxes (the same dots in every key, not every scale tone in a fret window). Natural minor uses those same boxes, parked on the relative major. Pentatonic and blues use the five pentatonic boxes, lowest complete shape on the neck first.
- Roots marked. On a blues shape, the blue note (♭5) is a ring, not a root.

## Out of scope

- CAGED chord variations on Chords
- Chord-tone highlighting inside a scale
- Modes, harmonic minor, melodic minor, arpeggios, tuner, fretboard quiz
- Any change to the private Skills scale card
