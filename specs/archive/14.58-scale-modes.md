## In scope

- Mode row under the title when the scale is major or a mode: Major, Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian, Locrian.
- Aeolian stays the minor button. Ionian is the Major button.
- Each mode uses the parent major’s five boxes. The gold root is the mode’s tonic.
- One line: “Same notes as F major.”
- On a shape: “Continues into the A shape at fret 3.” Notes in both boxes get an outer ring.
- The shape letter stays when the key or the mode changes.

## Out of scope

- Harmonic minor, melodic minor, 3-notes-per-string, 4-notes-per-string, two-octave diagrams.
- New fingering tables. Do not re-decode the CAGED chart.
- CAGED chords. Chord tones inside a scale.

## Notes

`PARENT_DEGREE` is semitones above the parent major. Phrygian is 4. Lydian is 5. Not the scale-degree number.
